### Yr Amser / The Time

<div class="container2">

**Faint o'r gloch ydy hi?** What time is it?
**Mae hi'n ...** It's ...

</div>

<div class="container">
<div class="col">

- 1:00 **un o'r gloch**
- 2:00 **dau o'r gloch**
- 3:00 **tri o'r gloch**
- 11:00 **un ar ddeg o'r gloch**
- 12:00 **ddeuddeg o'r gloch**
- 12:00 **ganol dydd**
- 0:00 **ganol nos**

+ **chwarter wedi ...** quarter past ...
+ **hanner awr wedi ...** half past ...
+ **chwarter i ...** quarter to ...

</div>
<div class="col">

- **yn y bore** in the morning
- **yn y prynhawn** in the afternoon
- **gyda'r nos** in the evening
- **yn y nos** in the night

+ **amser codi** getting up time
+ **amser ysgol** school time
+ **amser cinio** dinner time
+ **amser gwely** bed time

</div>
</div>

<div class="container">

- **_am_ naw o'r gloch** _at_ nine o'clock
- **_am_ hanner awr wedi deg** _at_ half past ten
</div>
